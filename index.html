About NutriBuddy

NutriBuddy is a youth-focused health and nutrition platform dedicated to helping young people make healthier food and lifestyle choices. We believe that good health starts with knowledge, and our mission is to make nutrition education simple, engaging, and accessible to everyone.

Our Features

🥗 Nutrition Tips

Easy tips to help you make healthier food choices.

🍎 Healthy Recipes

Simple and affordable meals anyone can prepare.

🧠 Fun Quizzes

Interactive quizzes that make learning enjoyable.

💧 Healthy Habit Challenges

Daily and weekly challenges that motivate users to drink more water, eat more fruits and vegetables, and stay active.

Supporting SDG 3

NutriBuddy supports SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being by promoting nutrition education, healthy habits, and better health awareness among young people.
